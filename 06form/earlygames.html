Early Video Games

Introduction

The first video games were simple but groundbreaking, and they created the foundation for the entire gaming industry.

Early games used basic graphics, limited sound, and simple controls, but they were still exciting for players.

Important Early Games

These games helped shape what video games would become in arcades, homes, and later systems.

Tennis for Two (1958)
An early playable game that used an oscilloscope display and helped show what electronic games could be.
Pong (1972)
One of the first popular arcade games, featuring simple table tennis gameplay.
Space Invaders (1978)
A classic arcade shooter that helped popularize video gaming worldwide.
Pac-Man (1980)
A maze arcade game that became one of the most iconic video games ever made.
Donkey Kong (1981)
A platform game that introduced Mario and helped make character-based games more popular.
Tetris (1984)
A puzzle game that became globally popular across many systems.

Types of Early Games

Most early games were either arcade games or home console games.

Arcade Games

  • Found in public spaces like arcades
  • Focused on simple controls and high scores
  • Examples include Pac-Man and Space Invaders

Home Consoles

  • Played on TVs at home
  • Included early systems like Atari
  • Used simple graphics and limited storage

Why They Matter

These early games created ideas and gameplay styles that are still used today.

Even though they looked simple, many early games were important because they helped gaming become popular with millions of people.
