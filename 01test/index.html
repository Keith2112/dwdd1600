Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship refers to the responsible, safe, and ethical use of digital technology—especially the internet, social media, and online platforms. It's about how people behave and interact in the digital world.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules, rights, and legal issues connected to the use of technology, computers, the internet, and digital information.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. It is a set of rules that explains how people are allowed to use a computer system, network, internet connection, or digital devices.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is using someone else's words, ideas, images, or work and pretending they are your own without giving proper credit.

In simple terms, it is copying without acknowledgment.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is any form of cheating or unfair behavior in school or educational work.

It happens when a student gains an unfair academic advantage or submits work that is not honestly their own.
