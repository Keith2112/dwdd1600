Use

Woodworking is used for shelves, tables, boxes, chairs, picture frames, cabinets, and small gifts. Good planning helps keep each project safe and clean.

Simple Safety Steps

  • Measure before cutting.
  • Wear eye protection.
  • Keep hands away from blades.
  • Sand with the grain when possible.
