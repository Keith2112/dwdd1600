About This Site

This website explores the history and evolution of video games from their early beginnings to modern gaming.

What You Will Learn

  • The rise of arcade and console gaming
  • How technology shaped modern games

Gaming Eras Overview

  1. Early Games (1960s to 1980s) Simple graphics and experimental gameplay
  2. Console Era (1980s to 2000s) Home systems and iconic franchises
  3. Modern Gaming (2000s to Present) Online play, realism, and massive worlds

Why Video Games Matter

Video games have become one of the most popular forms of entertainment in the world and have changed massively overtime.
