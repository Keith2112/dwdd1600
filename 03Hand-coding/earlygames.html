Early Video Games (1960s to 1980s)

Introduction

The first video games were simple but groundbreaking and created the foundation for the entire gaming industry.

Important Early Games

Tennis for Two (1958)
the first ever example of a playable video "game", it was created with transistors, relays, and a oscilloscope analog computer.
Pong (1972)
One of the first arcade games, featuring simple table tennis gameplay.
Space Invaders (1978)
A classic arcade shooter that helped popularize video gaming worldwide.
Pac-Man (1980)
A maze arcade game that became one of the most iconic video games ever made.
Donkey Kong (1981)
Introduced platform gameplay and the character Mario.
Tetris (1984)
A puzzle game that became globally popular across many systems.

Types of Early Games

Arcade Games

  • Found in public spaces like arcades
  • Simple controls and high-score focus
  • Examples: Pac-Man, Space Invaders

Home Consoles

  • Played on TVs at home
  • Early systems like Atari
  • Simple graphics and limited storage

Why They Matter

These early games created the foundation for modern gaming and introduced many gameplay styles still used today.
