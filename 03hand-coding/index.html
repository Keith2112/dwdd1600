About This Site

This website explores the history and evolution of video games from their early beginnings to modern gaming.

Video games started as simple experiments, but they became a major part of entertainment, technology, and culture.

What You Will Learn

On this site, you will learn about important moments in gaming history and how games changed over time.

  • How video games started in the 1960s
  • The rise of arcade and console gaming
  • How technology shaped modern games

Gaming Eras Overview

Gaming history can be divided into a few major eras that show how much the industry has grown.

  1. Early Games: Simple graphics and experimental gameplay from the 1960s to 1980s.
  2. Console Era: Home systems and iconic franchises from the 1980s to 2000s.
  3. Modern Gaming: Online play, realism, and massive worlds from the 2000s to today.

Why Video Games Matter

Video games have become one of the most popular forms of entertainment in the world and have changed massively over time.

Many games also tell stories, build communities, and give players new ways to be creative.
That is why gaming continues to grow every year.
